STATUS: TRAINED AND SMOKE-TESTED

The model completed training and the inference script generated a CSV for 2022.
Dataset rows matched to DES + IMD: 322 patch-year records.
Distinct district-season target groups: 75 (10 failures, 65 non-failures).
Grouped held-out-district evaluation: 20 district-season groups, 2 positive labels.
Accuracy: 0.90; majority-class baseline: 0.90.
Balanced accuracy: 0.7222; precision: 0.50; recall: 0.50; F1: 0.50.

Limitations: this is an exploratory runnable prototype. The training seasons 2019-2022 do not include a June-August mean ONI >= +0.5 C, so it has not learned/validated El Niño-specific crop-failure behavior. Holdout metrics are unstable because the test set has only 2 positive district-season groups.
